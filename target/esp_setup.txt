These are files that target/esp_sdk needs from tmp/esp_worm/. Without wifi.sh
esp_worm is built without a network and the board is not used.

* wifi.sh declares the network esp_worm joins and nothing else:
	ESP_WORM_WIFI_SSID="<ssid>"
	ESP_WORM_WIFI_PASSWORD="<password>"
* host.key, host.pub, device.key and device.pub are made when missing.
