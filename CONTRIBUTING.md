Ports to 1 or two more IoT devices are planned. Bringing up libhatchet on a
new dev board is easy because it only depends on libc and can be configured
to avoid unsupported the features.

Another nice feature to have would be TAP-protocol testing and timing support.

It is also easy to envision a dedicated telemetry tool. A sponsor would help
with that.
